## Verification and retained limits

**Executed checks:** catalogue generation, analytical boundary checks, structural integrity (35 matching records/notes/BibTeX entries and local links), and Git whitespace validation passed in this milestone. No deck source or rendered slide artifact was produced.

The argument script uses deterministic closed-form normal calculations and states the model, prior, fixed margins and losses. There are no random draws or empirical observations. The structural checker verifies registry/note fingerprints, bibliography keys, local links, source count and the closed gate. Neither script evaluates source truth, corpus representativeness or adequacy of a welfare criterion.

Retained work includes direct reading of the book, independent reconstruction of the AER coding dispute, full contemporary methods/proofs and replication data, and a broader comparative reading of the prioritised recent candidates. The 35-source catalogue remains heterogeneous in reading depth. The current targeted update is documented but not exhaustive.

This review advances the research argument; it does not certify all 35 sources as fully read, settle historical or prevalence claims, or satisfy the user's separate instruction to keep slides closed. Subsequent review should engage the retained disagreements rather than treat this record as a blanket approval.
