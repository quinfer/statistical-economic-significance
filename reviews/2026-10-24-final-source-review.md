## Objections and revisions

| Issue raised in reading or review | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Earlier notes treated the two final articles as inaccessible | Preserve failed-search chronology, then record supplied-file identity and actual reading. Remove current draft-only verdicts without rewriting the historical search outcome. |
| Darby combines test-driven restrictions with magnitude interpretation | Verify the productivity calculations and retained t = 0.31 control. Reject a blanket magnitude-neglect/universal-deletion characterisation; retain the specific oil-restriction and non-rejection questions. |
| Darby's growth units could be mistaken for relative percentages | Describe approximately −0.46/+0.61 **percentage-point adjustments to annual log growth**. |
| H&S's p. 315 explanation could be mistaken for a reported joint test | Distinguish its interval-containment reconstruction from a covariance-aware cross-equation test; Darby's n. 39 concerns different, within-regression restrictions. |
| Bernheim–Wantz final Table 4 differs from the draft | Record the final magnitude/standard-error panel and n. 17's direct tests of differences. Do not accuse the final article of merely comparing isolated significance labels. |
| Magnitude evidence could become a blanket methodological acquittal | Keep stability/equivalence, identification, alternative explanations and selection questions open. Note the substantive specification rationale without assuming it validates every choice. |
| H&S's “no inference” defence could be generalised to the entire rating paragraph | Limit it to the isolated Table 1 remark; the surrounding Table 2 discussion does draw a theory-directed inference. Record the JSE quotation's omitted Table 1 qualifier. |
| JSE and EJW table errors could be conflated | Final JSE retains the Q14/Q15 mismatch with the matrix, but its 1980s Q7 is 47.3%, consistent with the 1996 original. Do not infer a correction history from this version difference. |
| Registry's source-claim field mixed author reports with project findings | Separate the authors' reported results from the project's matrix/table comparisons in the core record and paper note. |
| Row totals and published cross-tabulation were ambiguously grouped | State that row totals reproduce the supplied matrix; the cross-tabulation reproduces both publications. |
| Aggregate book percentages might be treated as recoverable article codes | Explain the underdetermination and unverified assumptions about recoding, eligibility and applicable denominators. Do not manufacture the missing rows. |
