## Objections and revisions

| Objection | Applied revision |
|---|---|
| Saying the book “corrects” its empirical base overstates verification of the expanded matrix | The synthesis now says it **reports** a correction and expansion. The 137/187 boundary is explicit. |
| Missing item codes do not make new independent recoding impossible | Distinguish reproducing original classifications from constructing a new protocol using original articles. |
| The p. 78/Table 6.1 discrepancy is conditional on the same construct and universe | Retain an unresolved apparent numerical/construct tension, not an unconditional finding of a specific erroneous count. |
| A simulation criterion conditioned on selection establishes overlapping criteria, not statistical dependence by itself | Book ledger now says the criteria overlap. |
| Becker et al.'s Hausman discussion does not reconstruct every preceding selection decision | Limit its force to counterevidence against a blanket account of mechanically test-driven specification choice. |
| Final Darby differences were seen through H&S's quotations, not direct final reading | Attribute those differences explicitly to the quoted final text and retain draft-only substantive access. |
| Woodbury's take-up scenario is conditional | State complete take-up with unchanged benefit reductions; do not present it as an estimated scale-up effect. |
| Unread Table 6 and the detailed report might contain additional contrasts | Critique H&S's cited significance comparison without claiming that no contrasts were ever tested. An “only one group benefits” assertion additionally needs absence/negligibility evidence elsewhere. |
| Woodbury Table 7 was extracted, not visually inspected | Put that limitation beside the numerical table. |
| Lecture is not book; legal materiality is not a medical-causation finding | Separate source records and a primary-opinion audit; correct the lecture's *Basic* date to 1988. |

Root also rechecked two initially uncertain small numerals in the preview: p. 74 gives 50 contextual-comparison articles and p. 75 gives 66.5% for Q3. They agree with the retrieved original survey; no discrepancy is claimed for either. This illustrates why preliminary visual readings were not promoted into final findings without rechecking.
