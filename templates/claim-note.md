# C000: proposition

**Status:** open / qualified / contested / provisionally supported / rejected

**Statement type:** source claim / project interpretation / illustration

State one proposition and its quantifier, population and conditions.

## Evidence for and against

Link each relevant paper note, consulted locator, access depth and direction of support. Mark direct evidence, contextual relevance and interpretation separately.

## Assessment

Separate formal validity, empirical support and normative judgement. Identify the strongest objection and a concrete condition that would change the assessment.

## Connections

Link concepts, disagreements and synthesis.
