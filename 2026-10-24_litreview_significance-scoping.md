# Literature scoping memo: statistical versus economic significance

**Scope:** foundational and contemporary methodological debates relevant to economics and finance. **Milestone:** initial scaffold and purposive selection. **Corpus:** 32 works with checked bibliographic identity, not 32 fully reviewed papers. No claim of exhaustive search through October 2026.

The [literature map](sources/literature-map.md) contains the selected works and reading depths; the [search log](sources/search-log.md) records access barriers, version decisions, gaps and exclusions. The [wiki](wiki/index.md) connects source notes to concepts, claims and disagreements.

## Initial thematic map

The six proposed strands are the original economics controversy; interpretation and reform of inference; precision, power and interval meaning; meaningful thresholds and equivalence; decisions and identification; and finance applications with contested replication. See the [taxonomy](planning/literature-taxonomy.md) for selection rationale.

## Preliminary assessment

**Project interpretation:** the most productive question is not simply which type of significance matters more. The resource should distinguish the definition of importance, empirical support for a magnitude, and a rational action under uncertainty. The [argument framework](synthesis/argument-framework.md) treats this as a hypothesis-generating structure and lists counterexamples and proof obligations.

The strongest competing arguments concern substantive judgement, reliability of estimated magnitudes, removal or tightening of thresholds, interpretation of intervals, and consequences of decisions. The [positions map](wiki/disagreements/methodological-positions.md) links each reconstruction to consulted evidence and objections.
