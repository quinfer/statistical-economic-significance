# Reading and study resources

Begin with a question, then follow its evidence. The [learning map](learning-map.md) connects each curriculum module to a concept, a disputed claim, paper notes and an exercise. The [wiki index](../wiki/index.md) is the broader research entry point.

## Read the depth label first

The registry distinguishes `metadata_only`, `publisher_description`, `abstract_read`, `selected_sections_read` and `full_text_read`. A verified DOI identifies a work; it does not establish that the work supports a particular claim. A successful download does not mean its contents have been read.

The three papers in the 2008 exchange have full-text reading records. The book has a selected-excerpt record, not a full-book reading record. Other entries range from metadata to selected sections. The targeted [2024–2026 update](../sources/update-2024-2026.md) records candidates, versions and exclusions; it is not an exhaustive review of everything published in those years. Check the current registry rather than treating this page as a substitute for its dates and locators.

To use a source, open its paper note, identify the consulted version and passage, and follow the recorded URL when available. If access is unavailable to you, label your own reading accordingly. Do not turn a paraphrase from this wiki into a claim that you read the primary text.

## Keep a learning record

For each substantial claim, record four things:

1. **Statement:** what is claimed, in whose voice, and about which target?
2. **Evidence:** which source and locatable passage support that attribution?
3. **Interpretation:** what follows under which assumptions, and what does not?
4. **Challenge:** which counterargument or new evidence could change your judgement?

Use **Source claim**, **Project interpretation**, **Open question** and **Illustration** to keep these roles visible. Synthetic calculations are illustrations, even when their arithmetic is exact. Reproducing an aggregate from a supplied coding matrix verifies that calculation; it does not validate every underlying judgement.

## Where to extend the project

The [search log](../sources/search-log.md), [expanded-matrix follow-up](../wiki/evidence/expanded-matrix-followup.md) and final section of the [synthesis](../synthesis/critical-synthesis.md) identify unfinished work. Useful contributions include a newly accessible source with precise version information, a passage that changes a coding judgement, or a numerical counterexample that narrows a methodological claim.

Record unresolved access as an access limit. Keep copyrighted full texts out of the public repository unless there is an appropriate basis to redistribute them. Follow the [research standards](../AGENTS.md); adding a new result or completing an exercise does not reopen the slide gate.
