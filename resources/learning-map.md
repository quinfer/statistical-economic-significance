## Follow a disagreement without losing its scope

**Threshold reform:** compare [C005](../wiki/claims/C005-threshold-reforms.md) with [C007](../wiki/claims/C007-intervals.md). Replacing a cutoff with an interval changes presentation and available interpretation; it does not by itself settle identification, selection or the choice of an economic margin.

**Finance replication:** use [C008](../wiki/claims/C008-finance-replication.md) to compare the research designs before concluding that opposing headlines describe the same test. Add [uncertainty beyond sampling](../wiki/concepts/uncertainty-beyond-sampling.md) when analysis choices are part of the question.

**Optional historical case:** use the [2008 debate](../CURRICULUM.md#optional-case-study-the-2008-debate) to separate arguments about research practice, inference and decisions. The [AER audit](../sources/aer-audit/README.md) gives a route through the original articles, the recorded scores and the limits of the evidence.

## Check the argument as well as the calculation

The [checked mathematical examples](../wiki/evidence/mathematical-checks.md) and [executable worked examples](../implementations/worked_examples.py) give synthetic cases to inspect. They can expose an invalid universal statement or illustrate a conditional implication. They cannot estimate how often a practice occurs in economics or establish the value of an actual policy or trading strategy.
