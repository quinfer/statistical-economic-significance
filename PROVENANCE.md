# Provenance and review

Barry Quinn maintains this repository. Research organisation, source notes, synthesis drafts, exercises and code were developed with AI assistance in a supervised project. Retrieval, reading depth and review claims must be checked against the source records; agreement among agents is not a substitute for evidence.

The learning structure follows the maintainer's [Quantitative Finance Foundations](https://github.com/quinfer/quantitative-finance-foundations): quick start, staged curriculum, annotated readings and status page. The evidence-linked wiki follows the general [LLM wiki pattern](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) of persistent interconnected notes with source trails.

## Evidence and review

- Identities and reading limits are in the [core](sources/registry.json) and [supporting](sources/aer-audit/supporting-registry.json) registries.
- Agent remits, objections and revisions are in [reviews](reviews/review-gate.md). No human scholarly approval or independent empirical replication is claimed.
- The [learning review](reviews/2026-11-23-learning-review.md) concerns navigation, exercises, mathematics and publication readiness. It does not reopen the slide gate.
- Synthetic calculations are labelled, inspectable and tested; they do not extend the literature's empirical findings.

## Public history

The public repository starts with a portable learning snapshot. Earlier research commits are retained locally because they contain personal machine locations. In the public material, supplied-document filename references replace those paths. Hashes, reading locators, access limits and earlier failed-access records are preserved. A filename does not imply the file is distributed here. Historical `/tmp/` cache locations remain as non-personal provenance records; the public learning commands do not require those paths or files.

Copyrighted PDFs and full-text extracts are excluded. Optional audit checks can use privately obtained source artefacts; the documented learning commands run from public factual transcriptions without them.
