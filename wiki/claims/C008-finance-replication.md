# C008: The finance replication diagnosis is settled

**Status:** contested; no verdict. **Type:** empirical and methodological disagreement.

## Evidence trail

[Harvey–Liu–Zhu](../papers/harvey-liu-zhu-2016-cross-section.md), abstract, motivates stronger adjustment for searching. [McLean–Pontiff](../papers/mclean-pontiff-2016-predictability.md), abstract, studies return decay. [Jensen–Kelly–Pedersen](../papers/jensen-kelly-pedersen-2023-replication.md), introduction and selected model/results passages, reaches a more optimistic assessment. [Harvey–Liu 2020](../papers/harvey-liu-2020-false-missed.md), opening discussion, includes missed discovery costs.

**Project interpretation:** compare sample construction, factor universes, dependence, risk adjustment, economic targets and inferential models before deciding which findings conflict. Out-of-sample decay can reflect selection, learning, changing opportunities or combinations; its existence does not identify one mechanism by itself.

**Next test:** build a matched comparison of definitions of replication and the assumptions needed to connect statistical discovery to net investor benefit. No empirical results have been independently reproduced here.
