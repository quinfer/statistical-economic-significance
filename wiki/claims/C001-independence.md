# C001: Economic significance independently of inference

**Status:** open, with the proposition decomposed. **Type:** project interpretation.

“Economic significance can be established independently of statistical inference” is underspecified. A benchmark can be defined without testing the present sample. Judging an observed number consequential is descriptive. Claiming an unknown population effect exceeds that benchmark needs an evidential argument. Choosing an action requires a further decision criterion.

## Evidence trail

- [Hoover–Siegler rejoinder](../papers/hoover-siegler-2008-rejoinder.md), pp. 57–59: relevant objection to treating uncertain magnitudes as established.
- [McCloskey–Ziliak reply](../papers/mccloskey-ziliak-2008-reply.md), pp. 50, 52: relevant case for substantive judgement; not simply a monetary-payoff rule.
- [Imbens](../papers/imbens-2021.md), abstract: distinctions between inferential tasks.
- [Kandel–Stambaugh](../papers/kandel-stambaugh-1996-asset-allocation.md), abstract: decision relevance is not a conventional rejection prerequisite.

**Critical test:** attempt counterexamples involving known accounting quantities, finite-population description, partial identification and robustly dominant actions. “Inference” must be defined without making the proposition tautological. Do not substitute “p < .05” for “inference”.

## Close-reading update

**Conditional conclusion:** independence holds for defining a benchmark and may hold for a fully observed descriptive target. It does not generally hold for establishing the magnitude of an unknown population or causal effect from noisy data. Action can precede such establishment under a specified decision rule. The [substantive synthesis](../../synthesis/critical-synthesis.md) now develops these distinctions and their counterexamples.

**Counterargument retained:** under a compatible one-sided procedure, a lower bound above a positive economic margin also exceeds zero. Thus a specific definition of established positive importance entails zero-null rejection. See [C011](C011-nested-meaningful-tests.md). This prevents the broad claim that rejection can never be necessary in any sense.
