# Confidence intervals

**Working distinction.** Frequentist coverage is a property of a procedure over hypothetical repetitions under its assumptions. A realised interval does not automatically carry a posterior probability for its fixed parameter. Bayesian credible intervals answer a different probability question and depend on a prior and likelihood.

**Source trail:** [Greenland et al.](../papers/greenland-et-al-2016.md) provides practical interpretation; [Morey et al.](../papers/morey-et-al-2016-intervals.md) offers a stronger critique of common justifications. [Neyman](../papers/neyman-1937-estimation.md) is a verified bibliographic foundation awaiting substantive reading, not evidence already examined for the theory.

**Project interpretation:** comparing an interval with meaningful economic thresholds can expose uncertainty that a star obscures. This does not repair misspecification, identification failure or selection. Inverting compatible tests and presenting intervals can share mathematics while encouraging different substantive discussion. Neither representation is automatically superior for every question.
