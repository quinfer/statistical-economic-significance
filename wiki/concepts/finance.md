# Finance: economic value, discovery and replication

**Project framework.** A return predictor's coefficient, an estimated alpha, a risk-adjusted portfolio payoff and an investor's utility improvement are different targets. Economic relevance can depend on risk, implementation costs, constraints, horizon, capacity and parameter uncertainty.

**Competing evidence trails:** [Harvey–Liu–Zhu](../papers/harvey-liu-zhu-2016-cross-section.md) emphasises extensive searching; [McLean–Pontiff](../papers/mclean-pontiff-2016-predictability.md) examines changes in returns outside the original sample and after publication. [Jensen–Kelly–Pedersen](../papers/jensen-kelly-pedersen-2023-replication.md) offers a more optimistic replication analysis. These designs and estimands must be compared before calling them direct contradictions.

[Harvey–Liu 2020](../papers/harvey-liu-2020-false-missed.md) gives missed discoveries a role alongside false discoveries. [Kandel–Stambaugh](../papers/kandel-stambaugh-1996-asset-allocation.md) concerns choice under Bayesian parameter uncertainty. None of these initial readings establishes a profitable implementable strategy for the reader.

## Analysis choices and economic interpretation

[Menkveld et al. 2024](../papers/menkveld-etal-2024-nonstandard-errors.md), selected abstract, introduction and sections II.D–III, adds analyst variation on common data. **Project implication:** compare estimands, analysis paths and assumptions before treating one precise coefficient as a stable economic magnitude. Dispersion across analysts does not automatically quantify bias or provide a variance component to add to every standard error. See [uncertainty beyond sampling](uncertainty-beyond-sampling.md).

[Mitton 2024 and other candidates](../../sources/update-2024-2026.md) remain explicit next readings. Mitton's issue year is 2024 but its first online date is 2022; its standardised-effect proposal has only abstract-level support here. No implementable trading strategy or realised investor welfare is inferred.
