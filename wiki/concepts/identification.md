# Identification and transportability

**Project distinction.** Precision concerns how tightly a procedure estimates its target under its assumptions. Identification concerns what the observed distribution can reveal about that target under substantive and design assumptions. Transportability concerns using results in another population or setting. A narrow interval does not by itself answer the latter questions.

**Source trail:** selected passages in [Deaton–Cartwright](../papers/deaton-cartwright-2018-rcts.md) motivate separating randomisation, precision and application elsewhere. This is a critique of automatic guarantees, not a general rejection of experiments. [Manski 2021](../papers/manski-2021-econometrics-decisions.md) motivates examining states outside a chosen model.

**Open question:** how should the synthesis handle partial identification, confounding, non-stationarity and equilibrium responses? The initial corpus needs a more specialised economics source before detailed teaching claims on these methods are made.
