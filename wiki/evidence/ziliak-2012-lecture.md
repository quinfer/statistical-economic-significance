## Remaining work

Audit the medical and unemployment examples against their primary sources before making causal claims about jobs or lives. Do not import this source's quotations into future teaching as independently verified historical quotations.

## Primary-opinion follow-up

The [bounded court audit](matrixx-2011-lecture-audit.md) confirms that *Matrixx* concerns sufficient pleading, keeps statistical significance potentially relevant, and requires contextual evidence beyond bare adverse-event reports. It does not adjudicate pharmaceutical causation or liability on the merits. It also corrects slide 23: *Basic v. Levinson* dates to 1988, while the earlier *TSC Industries* case dates to 1976. The lecture itself distinguishes the Court's holding from Ziliak's stronger position on slide 26. Individual oral-argument quotations remain unchecked.
