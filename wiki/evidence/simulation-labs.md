# Simulation evidence boundary

**Illustration / project derivation — 23 November 2026.** The [three simulation labs](../../simulations/README.md) extend the learning pathway with seeded random experiments, exact normal-model benchmarks, Monte Carlo error and worked solutions. They supply evidence that the implementation behaves as derived under its stipulated models. They are not new empirical evidence about economists, policy effects, asset returns or the disputed AER codes.

| Lab | What the experiment can establish under its assumptions | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| [Power](../../simulations/labs/01-power.md) | Rejection, equivalence and coverage probabilities for an unbiased known-SE normal mean; consequences of understating that SE | A substantively correct margin, a valid causal design, or calibration under an unmodelled dependence structure |
| [Selection](../../simulations/labs/02-selection.md) | Conditional sign/magnitude risks under a specified filter; independent-holdout comparisons; family error in an independent all-null search | Actual publication incentives, real factor-discovery bias, time-varying effects or the truth of an empirical replication claim |
| [Decisions](../../simulations/labs/03-decisions.md) | Expected utility and oracle regret for binary actions under declared priors, state distributions and losses | The right prior, welfare weights or cost; universal dominance across states; robust Bayes or minimax-regret optimality |

The [reference outputs](../../simulations/results/summary.md) retain denominators and Monte Carlo errors. Conditional metrics use selected counts; undefined ratios and empty subsets are not reported as zero. Policies share simulated states within each scenario so differences are paired. Null, strong-effect, uncertainty-misspecification and prior-mismatch cases prevent one deliberately favourable calibration from carrying the lesson.

**Source claims remain separate.** Follow [power and selection](../concepts/power-selection.md), [equivalence](../concepts/equivalence.md) and [decisions](../concepts/decisions.md) to the existing source notes and their passage locators. The labs do not change reading-depth labels, extend selected book passages, recover the 187-paper matrix or supply missing source verification.

**Open questions:** how would conclusions change under dependent observations, estimated variance, uncertain costs, heterogeneous targets, endogenous selection, different welfare weights or a defensible ambiguity set? A response needs a new model and validation, not a change of terminology in the current output.
