## Consequence for the argument

**Project judgement:** Direct reading strengthens the balanced reconstruction. The book offers a substantive-judgement programme that explicitly includes uncertainty concepts, and it recognises some improvements. It also contains claims requiring more careful reconciliation than a global prevalence slogan allows. Neither the existence of those problems nor the disputed cases invalidates every criticism of mechanical significance.

The immediate audit can assess attribution, arithmetic and selected interpretations. It cannot yet deliver a corrected population misuse rate, a full-book historical verdict, or a causal estimate of jobs, lives or welfare lost. The [coding ledger](aer-coding-audit.md) and case ledgers for [Darby/Woodbury–Spiegelman](aer-darby-woodbury.md) and [monetary transmission/dividends/addiction](aer-macro-finance-addiction.md) identify exactly which propositions have improved evidence.
