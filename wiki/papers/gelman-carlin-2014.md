## Source claim

The authors recommend design analyses of wrong-sign and magnitude-exaggeration risks, particularly for statistically significant estimates in noisy studies. Their Type S and expected Type M quantities are conditional on statistical significance. Prospective and retrospective calculations should use externally justified plausible effect sizes; retrospective design analysis differs from observed power obtained by reusing the current study's estimate.

## Project interpretation

Shows why reporting estimated magnitude does not resolve uncertainty: selected noisy estimates may exaggerate effects or have the wrong sign.
