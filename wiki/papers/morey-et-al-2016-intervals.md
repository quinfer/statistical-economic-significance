## Source claim

The authors argue that frequentist coverage alone does not justify common interpretations of a realised interval as a probability statement about its parameter or a ranking of plausible values. They challenge broad interval advocacy and propose other foundations for interval estimation.

## Project interpretation

A strong objection to treating confidence intervals as a complete methodological solution; its examples and philosophical conclusions require separate evaluation.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Abstract and introduction inspected, not all counterexamples or proposed alternatives. Record the authors' argument without treating it as settled consensus.
