The authors report a survey of 137 papers and about 82% adverse classifications on the decisive-significance item. Final JSE Table 5, p. 542, reports 112 adverse Q16 classifications. The paper acknowledges improvements while asserting deterioration on its principal criteria; pp. 532–533 exclude significance-screened simulations from a positive Q17 code.
