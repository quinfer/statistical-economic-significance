## Source claim

The ASA statement regards p-values as potentially useful but insufficient on their own. It urges contextual scientific reasoning and discusses estimation, Bayesian inference, decision theory and other alternatives, each requiring assumptions. Its contextual introduction records the statement's formal ASA approval and acknowledges disagreements and topics deliberately omitted, including power.

## Project interpretation

Provides an institutional baseline for separating significance, substantive importance and wider scientific reasoning without presenting all statistical reform proposals as ASA policy.
