## Source claim

Schuirmann compares a power-based approach with two one-sided tests for bioequivalence. The abstract relates TOST to a 1−2α confidence interval and reports favourable error properties relative to the power approach under the studied conditions.

## Project interpretation

A formal route for testing practical equivalence, contingent on a defensible margin and statistical model.
