## Source claim

The authors propose changing the default significance threshold from .05 to .005 for claims of new discoveries in fields using .05. They argue that prevailing standards of statistical evidence are too weak and that the change would improve reproducibility. This is a reform proposal rather than proof of universal benefits across research settings.

## Project interpretation

Represents a strong alternative to abolishing threshold categories and motivates examination of discovery standards, error trade-offs, costs and selection incentives.
