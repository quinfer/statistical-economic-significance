## Source claim

The working paper reassesses null claims using equivalence tests and proposes meaningful-margin testing. It distinguishes failure to establish equivalence from evidence of a large effect (p. 22), and generic audit margins from appropriate study-specific margins (p. 34).

## Project interpretation

Connects contemporary economics practice to the 2008 debate while exposing a crucial gap between detecting inadequate evidence for negligibility and measuring false conclusions about true effects. Our assessment: the broader interpretation in terms of Type II errors needs qualification; it is not an observed truth rate.

## Limits and unresolved questions
