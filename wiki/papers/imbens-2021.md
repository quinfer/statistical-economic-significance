## Source claim

Imbens distinguishes decisions under uncertainty, for which estimates and uncertainty intervals are often more informative, from questions where testing a null hypothesis is a natural goal and p-values appropriate. He opposes general bans and links excessive emphasis on significance to p-hacking and publication bias, discussing pre-analysis plans and replication among possible responses.

## Project interpretation

Provides an economics-specific bridge between inference and decisions and challenges both routine significance reporting and blanket abolition.
