## Source claim

The authors develop a hierarchical Bayesian model and international factor evidence that lead them to a substantially more favourable assessment of factor replication than earlier critiques. Their discussion attributes differences to sample construction, risk adjustment, the definition of replication and treatment of multiple testing.

## Project interpretation

Supplies a recent substantive counterposition to pessimistic finance-replication claims and blanket threshold tightening.
