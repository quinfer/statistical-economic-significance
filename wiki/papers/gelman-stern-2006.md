## Source claim

Comparing two significance classifications does not directly test the difference between their estimates. The authors demonstrate that substantially different p-values can coexist with a poorly determined difference. They distinguish this error from the separate problems of arbitrary thresholds and confusing statistical significance with practical importance, and explicitly focus their objection on comparing significance levels.

## Project interpretation

Supports training on comparing effects across groups, models and studies, including the error of interpreting significance in only one group as evidence of heterogeneity.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Selected sections inspected. The title should be interpreted as 'not necessarily significant', not as claiming that a difference between such estimates can never be statistically significant.
