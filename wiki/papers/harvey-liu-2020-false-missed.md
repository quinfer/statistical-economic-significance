## Source claim

The article proposes a double-bootstrap approach to calibrating false and missed discoveries while allowing different costs for the two kinds of error. Its opening discussion argues that attention to test power can change conclusions about manager selection.

## Project interpretation

Provides a finance-specific bridge between multiple testing, power and the economic costs of decision errors.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Opening sections only, not a full methodological review. Their definition of manager skill as positive alpha is explicitly distinguished from alternative equilibrium definitions.
