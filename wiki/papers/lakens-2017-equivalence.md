## Source claim

Lakens distinguishes non-rejection of a zero null from evidence of equivalence within chosen bounds. The introduction and Figure 1 discuss four combinations of difference and equivalence test outcomes, including an effect that is statistically different from zero yet practically equivalent.

## Project interpretation

Makes the difference between a zero threshold and a practical margin teachable without abandoning uncertainty assessment.
