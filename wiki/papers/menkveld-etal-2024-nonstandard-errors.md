## Source claim

The authors distinguish sampling uncertainty from variation across analysts choosing analysis paths on the same data. Their experiment finds substantial dispersion and reports that peer feedback reduces it. They caution that dispersion among published results may be smaller, while publication selection can introduce bias.

## Project interpretation

Challenges whether economic magnitudes and conventional uncertainty intervals remain stable across defensible analytical choices.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Selected sections only; complete methods, supplements and code not audited. Dispersion does not identify bias or show all analysis paths equally defensible. Do not treat it as an automatic additive correction to a standard error.
