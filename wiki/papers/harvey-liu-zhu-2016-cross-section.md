## Source claim

The authors argue that extensive factor searching makes conventional significance criteria inadequate. They introduce a multiple-testing framework and advocate a higher evidential hurdle for new factors, drawing a pessimistic conclusion about the reliability of claimed findings.

## Project interpretation

Tests whether a substantively large estimated effect can still be misleading after extensive searching and selection.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Abstract only. The pessimistic conclusion is the authors' argument under their framework, not an independently established fact or universal rule for all finance research.
