## Source claim

The authors separate substantive importance from precision, defend model-based inference and specification testing, and question a universal loss-function requirement. They allow loss-sensitive responses to weak signals (p. 15), acknowledge that omitted papers need not change survey conclusions (n. 3), and disclose a corpus mismatch in their interval-use comparison (n. 21).

## Project interpretation

Our interpretation: this strengthens the inference-versus-decision distinction while exposing technical and empirical claims that require scrutiny; useful uncertainty assessment does not establish a universal significance prerequisite.
