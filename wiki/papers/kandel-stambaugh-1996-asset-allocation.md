## Source claim

The paper considers a risk-averse Bayesian investor allocating between stocks and cash and updating beliefs about a predictive regression. Its abstract states that current predictor values can materially affect portfolio choice even when conventional statistical measures describe the regression relation as weak and prior beliefs weigh against predictability.

## Project interpretation

Directly distinguishes decision relevance from conventional statistical evidence while retaining updating and uncertainty.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Abstract only. The result does not establish economic magnitudes independently of statistical reasoning or justify ignoring estimation uncertainty.
