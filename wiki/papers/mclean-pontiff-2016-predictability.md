## Source claim

The authors compare return predictability outside original study samples and after publication. They report declining performance and interpret their evidence as consistent with both data-mining effects and investors learning about mispricing from academic publications.

## Project interpretation

Introduces out-of-sample economic performance and market adaptation as distinct explanations relevant to replication.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Abstract only; numerical findings are intentionally omitted at the starter stage. Performance decay does not by itself establish that the original predictive relation was wholly spurious.
