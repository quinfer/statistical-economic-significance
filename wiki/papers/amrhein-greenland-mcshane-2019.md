## Source claim

The authors call for ending conventional categorical significance claims, not banning p-values, and explicitly allow specialised decision applications. They recommend discussing the practical implications of point estimates and interval limits while retaining statistical-assumption caveats. Failure to cross a significance threshold does not establish absence of an effect, and different significance classifications alone do not establish conflict between studies.

## Project interpretation

Supplies a prominent anti-dichotomisation argument with explicit limits and links practical interpretation to uncertainty rather than to point estimates alone.
