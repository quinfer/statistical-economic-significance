## Source claim

The authors propose removing statistical significance from its threshold screening role and considering continuously interpreted statistical information alongside design, prior evidence, mechanisms and costs and benefits. They explicitly do not advocate banning p-values.

## Project interpretation

Distinguishes abandoning dichotomous evidential gates from abandoning statistical evidence or uncertainty assessment.
