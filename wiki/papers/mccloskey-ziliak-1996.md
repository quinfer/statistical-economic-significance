## Source claim

The authors describe a 19-item survey of 182 AER papers, permitting yes/no/not-applicable answers. Table 1 reports 80.2% discussing coefficient sizes but 29.7% considering more than statistical significance decisive. Their broader diagnosis of confusion therefore concerns argumentative criteria as well as the presence of magnitudes. These are the authors' classifications, not independently verified prevalence.
