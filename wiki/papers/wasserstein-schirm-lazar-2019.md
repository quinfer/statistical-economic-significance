## Source claim

The editors advocate ending categorical significance labels while retaining appropriately interpreted continuous p-values. They recommend uncertainty measures alongside estimates and attention to substantive context, and warn against recreating dichotomies with confidence intervals or Bayes factors. The article explicitly represents the editors' individual scientific views, not an endorsed ASA position.

## Project interpretation

Offers a strong anti-dichotomisation position while distinguishing abandonment of significance labels from abandoning inference, and separates editorial advocacy from the official 2016 statement.
