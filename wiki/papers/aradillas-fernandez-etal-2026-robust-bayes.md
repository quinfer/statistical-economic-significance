## Source claim

Under their partial-identification framework and specified prior class, ex ante and ex post robust Bayes rules can differ, and randomisation can be optimal under both criteria. The conclusion acknowledges a restrictive prior and leaves the comparative merits of the criteria open.

## Project interpretation

A counterweight to treating decision theory as a unique automatic replacement for testing: the choice of criterion and information assumptions remains consequential.

## Limits and unresolved questions

Selected sections only; no proof verification or application replication. Normal signal with known covariance, linear mappings, convex symmetric parameter set and a particular two-point reduced-form prior limit generality. Results do not show that every decision requires randomisation or that one criterion is universally superior.
