## Source claim

The authors argue that randomisation does not automatically guarantee precise estimates or justify extrapolation. They distinguish unbiasedness, precision and inference, and discuss the additional knowledge required to apply trial results elsewhere. They expressly disavow a general argument against randomised trials.

## Project interpretation

Connects economic relevance to estimand choice, causal identification, heterogeneity and applicability beyond the study setting.
