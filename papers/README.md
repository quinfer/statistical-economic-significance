**Project interpretation — how to read.** Begin with an explicit question. Write down the estimand, units, uncertainty assumptions and decision criterion before deciding whether a paper answers it. Read the strongest linked objection alongside the argument you find persuasive. End each reading memo with one proposition the consulted text supports, one proposition it does not establish, and one observation that would change your assessment.

**Reading status matters.** A verified citation is not a completed reading. The guides reproduce the registry's coverage labels and identify particularly important access and version limits. Only the three articles in the 2008 exchange currently have `full_text_read` status; the book has selected-page coverage. The guide is a learning route through a developing research resource, not a claim that all 35 contributions have been fully assessed. For exact URLs, consulted sections, access dates and unresolved issues, follow each paper note to the registry.

The supporting AER papers, source versions and lecture are separate from the 35-work core. An earlier draft and its final article do not become two independent contributions. No source PDFs or extracted full texts are supplied here; use the links in the notes and lawful library access where needed.

The [slide gate](../reviews/review-gate.md) remains closed. Reading guides and written exercises do not authorise a Quarto presentation or imply scholarly approval of the underlying literature.
