# Staged work plan

## 1. Establish the literature

Complete the reading/access audit of the initial catalogue; obtain lawful access to unread books and articles where necessary. Conduct a matched reading of the 2008 exchange before accepting either side's account of the other. Trace the AER coding dispute to original article lists, coding rules and disputed examples. Update the search to 2024–2026 and examine citation chains. Record substitutions rather than silently changing the corpus.

**Exit condition:** 25–35 verified works with explicit versions, source-linked characterisations and coverage rationale. Every substantive claim used in synthesis has sufficient consulted evidence. Unread sources may remain in the bibliography, but cannot bear substantive argumentative weight.

## 2. Develop the wiki

Expand the starter paper notes into critical notes at the stated reading depth. Refine concepts and give each material disagreement a claim record. Record whether a source supports, opposes or merely contextualises a proposition. Maintain backlinks and an append-only research log.

**Exit condition:** no important conclusion rests on an unverified quotation, inaccessible chapter, unexplained economic threshold or conflation of tests with inference generally.

## 3. Write and critically review the synthesis

Use the [argument framework](../synthesis/argument-framework.md). Evaluate the strongest alternatives, including cases where decisions do not depend on significance, large estimates are too uncertain to support magnitude claims, and economic importance is not a single scalar threshold. Commission an adversarial review of both source fidelity and the argument. Revise and record the decision in the [gate](../reviews/review-gate.md).

**Exit condition:** reviewed synthesis with traceable evidence, explicit assumptions, retained disagreements and a recorded review decision. Do not equate bibliographic verification with this review.

## 4. Build slides after the gate

Develop the [training brief](../training/README.md) into Quarto reveal.js slides only after the recorded review requirements and the user's continuing hold are resolved. The [Markdown learning pathway](../CURRICULUM.md), added on 23 November 2026, already proposes prerequisites and exercises; it does not authorise a deck. Agree slide-session duration when that stage opens. Check calculations, rendering, accessibility and citations. Use examples with different conclusions so that learners practise reasoning rather than reproduce a predetermined verdict.
